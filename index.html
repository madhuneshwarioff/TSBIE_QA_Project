Project Story

The goal of this QA project was to assess whether a student can successfully move through the public admission journey, from discovering the admission button on the homepage to completing the process after downloading the form.

Testing Scope

The review covered homepage navigation, admission page access, download button behavior, absence of upload and submit actions, lack of instructions, broken post-download flow, mobile responsiveness, and general UI trust concerns including the invalid certificate warning on the external download destination.

Final Conclusion

The first part of the journey works: users can see the admission CTA and open the admission page. However, the core business flow is incomplete because users cannot upload or submit anything, receive no guidance, and are pushed toward an untrusted external download host. The website is not ready for a dependable end-to-end online admission experience in its current form.

Major Findings

These were the most important issues found during testing and they are the main reason the flow was marked as not production-ready.

High Severity

No Upload Option

Users can download the form, but there is no visible way to upload the completed form back to the website.

Critical Severity

No Submit Button

The admission flow has no final action that allows the user to submit an application online.

Medium Severity

No Instructions

The page does not tell users what to do after downloading the form or how to complete the journey.

High Severity

Untrusted Download Host

The provided evidence shows the download link landing on a privacy warning with certificate mismatch.
